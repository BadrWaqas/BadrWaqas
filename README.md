# Badr Waqas

Data Science student at NCI Dublin. I moved here from the UAE at 18 and mostly learn by building things about the city I ended up in.

I like real, awkward data — live APIs that only give you *now*, government CSVs with encoding problems, four years of my own bad chess moves. Everything here runs, and the READMEs say what didn't work too.

**[dublin-bikes-analysis](https://github.com/BadrWaqas/dublin-bikes-analysis)** — a pipeline that's been polling Dublin Bikes every 5 minutes for weeks, because the API only ever tells you the present tense. Cleaned into a time series, served through a Streamlit dashboard.

**[irish-housing-ml](https://github.com/BadrWaqas/irish-housing-ml)** — 797k real Irish property sales in SQLite, explored with SQL, then regression models that explain about 43% of price variation. The write-up spends as much time on why the other 57% is missing.

**[chess-self-analysis](https://github.com/BadrWaqas/chess-self-analysis)** — 1,488 of my own games through the Chess.com API and Stockfish. I don't study openings; turns out I've played the same one 98% of the time for four years.
